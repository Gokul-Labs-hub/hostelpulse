Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Who we are

HostelPulse is a tool that helps residents of the Shiv Nadar University Chennai hostel confirm their nightly attendance. It is a student-built prototype and is not an official service of the university unless the administration says so in writing. Questions about your data can be sent to info@snuchennai.edu.in.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, student number and room, taken from the hostel’s student list, and how you sign in. If you use Google, we receive only your name and email address.
  • Attendance: for each night, whether you were marked present, when, and the outcome of our checks.
  • Location: when you mark attendance, your phone shares one location reading. We use it to work out how far you are from the campus boundary and how accurate the reading is. We keep only those two numbers, not your coordinates.
  • Network: we check whether your connection comes from the college network. We keep only the result, matched or not matched. We do not store your network address.
  • Camera: used live to scan the QR code. No photos or video are saved or sent.
  • Requests: the reasons you write in missed-night requests.
  • Fingerprint and Face ID: if you add a device, we store a public key and a label for it. Your fingerprint or face never leaves your phone.
  • Staff actions: reviews and changes made by wardens and administrators are recorded with their account.
  • Sign-in protection: for a few minutes during fingerprint sign-in we keep a scrambled form of your network address to limit abuse.

Why we use it

To record attendance, to flag unusual attempts for a warden to review, to keep an accurate history, and to keep the service secure.

Automated checks

Each attempt receives a risk level based on the checks above. A flag never decides on its own that you were absent or that you did something wrong. A warden reviews every flagged attempt.

Who can see your data

You can see your own records. Wardens and administrators of the hostel can see attendance, flags and requests. We do not sell data, show ads or use advertising trackers. We rely on these service providers to run HostelPulse: Supabase (database, sign-in and server functions), Vercel (website hosting), Google (only if you choose Google sign-in) and our email provider (for sign-in codes).

On your device

Your browser keeps your sign-in session so you stay signed in. We use no tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

Attendance records and missed-night requests are kept for 2 years. Check details and risk flags are kept for 90 days. Records of staff actions are kept for 1 year. After these periods the data is deleted automatically.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by writing to info@snuchennai.edu.in. You can remove a saved device inside the app at any time. If you do not allow location access, your attendance is sent to a warden for review.

Security

Connections are encrypted. Access is limited by role, and staff actions are logged.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
