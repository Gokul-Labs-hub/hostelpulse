About HostelPulse

HostelPulse is a tool that helps record nightly attendance for residents of the Shiv Nadar University Chennai hostel. It is a student-built prototype and is not an official service of the university unless the administration says so in writing. Unless the hostel administration states otherwise in writing, the official attendance record remains the hostel’s own process.

Your account

Use only your own registered account. Do not share your sign-in codes, your phone or your access with anyone.

Fair use

  • Mark attendance only for yourself, and only when you are physically on campus.
  • Do not photograph, copy or forward the hostel QR code for others to use.
  • Do not use fake location tools, network tricks or any method to give false information.
  • Do not try to access other people’s data, or to break or overload the service.

Checks and review

Some attempts are flagged for review. A flag is not an accusation. A warden reviews it and decides. Please give honest reasons in missed-night requests.

Availability

HostelPulse is provided as it is and may sometimes be unavailable. If a problem stops you from marking attendance, use the missed-night request in the app.

Suspension

The administration may suspend an account that is used against these terms.
